Prepaid Allowance Wallets

Digital wallets auto-provisioned upon registration. Strict financial precision via BigDecimal preventing floating-point rounding errors.

Vouchers & QR Redemptions

Issue subsidized single or bulk vouchers with validity windows. Commuters display scannable Base64 QR payloads for instant taxi ride settlements.

Zero Overbooking Defenses

Hibernate @Version concurrency controls with exponential backoff retries guarantee zero overbooked seats and zero duplicate redemptions.

Immutable Audit Ledger

Strictly append-only financial accounting. Every wallet credit, booking debit, and cancellation refund logs a permanent audit entry.

Cancellation & Auto-Refunds

Commuters can cancel scheduled transit bookings before departure, instantly replenishing trip seat capacity and refunding 100% of the fare.

Operational Analytics

Real-time visual monitoring with Chart.js: voucher conversion funnels, daily spend velocity, route revenues, and seat load factors.
